Base URL is pre-filled with your live site (musicbingo123.netlify.app) so QR codes work out of the box. Only change it if you move the game to a different domain — use "Reset to default" to go back. QR codes only work if players' phones can actually reach the link, so a file:// path won't scan.

Rounds: each QR code bakes in several unique cards for that player. When the host starts a new round, players just tap "Next Round" on their own card — no rescanning needed. More rounds means a denser, slightly harder-to-scan QR code, so 5–8 rounds is a good default.
